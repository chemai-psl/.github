


# ChemAI Grand Programme, PSL University, Paris

Organization base of the ChemAI Grand Programme of PSL University, Paris. More information can be found at the [official website](https://www.chemai.psl.eu/).

Additional websites and underlying repositories are covering

- Bayesian Optimization
- Data Management
- Electronic Lab Notebooks

for people interested in those subjects.
If you are part of ChemAI or PSL university, feel free to reach out if you need any support or got questions.

## Bayesian Optimization

If you are interested in Bayesian Optimization we recommend to start with the [BO materials collection](https://chemai-psl.github.io/bo.materials/) and then explore the BO related repositories linked to this organization.

If you are part of ChemAI or PSL university, feel free to reach out if you need any support or got questions.

## Data Management

If you are interested in data management we recommend to start with the materials, courses and events provided by [nfdi4chem](https://nfdi4chem.de).

If you are part of ChemAI or PSL university, feel free to reach out if you need any support or got questions.

## Electronic Lab Notebooks

If you are interested in data management we recommend to start with having a look at [elabftw](https://www.elabftw.net/) and [chemotion](https://chemotion.net/). If you are part of ChemAI or PSL university, feel free to reach out if you need any support or got questions especially with regards to hosting your own version and implementing it in the culture of your institution.
